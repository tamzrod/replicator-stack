## Final Answer

**Which came first - the chicken or the egg?**

**Answer**: **The egg came first** (with qualifications)

**Scientific basis**: The first chicken hatched from an egg laid by a non-chicken (proto-chicken) parent. The genetic mutation defining "chicken-ness" occurred in the DNA of that egg before the chicken existed.

**Key qualifications**:
- If "chicken egg" = egg laid BY a chicken → chicken may have come first
- If "egg" = any egg producing a chicken → egg definitively came first
- Evolutionarily → eggs existed 150M years before chickens
