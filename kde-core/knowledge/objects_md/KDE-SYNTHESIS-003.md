## Definition

KDE can use its own Engineering Knowledge Repository to analyze and recommend improvements to the KDE methodology itself, through meta-investigation that treats KDE as the system under investigation.

## Meta-Knowledge Discovery

### HOW KDE EVOLVES

**Statement**: KDE evolves through accumulated investigation evidence that reveals patterns, which trigger capability improvements, which enable more sophisticated investigations.

**Evidence**: Analysis of 6 investigations (INV-015 to INV-020) shows consistent improvement cycle.

### WHY KDE EVOLVES

**Statement**: KDE evolves because each investigation generates traceable evidence that reveals both successes and gaps, creating a self-reinforcing improvement cycle.

**Evidence**: Each investigation referenced previous work, building cumulative understanding.

### WHAT IMPROVES KDE

**Statement**: Trace enforcement, validation gates, and evidence-weighted confidence consistently improve KDE by ensuring quality and enabling reliable knowledge extraction.

**Evidence**: Meta-pattern analysis identified these three factors in all successful investigations.
